Lore

The Liron Museum, the place where children learned about different things... from history, astrology, computing, biology, cooking, gardening and much more. There was a character that represented the subject for each area of the museum.

But after the budget ran out... the animators quit... and the show failed...

The place was closed.

The workers at the site were laid off.

The toons were left alone.

Now it's up to you to uncover the secrets of this place.

Be careful not to go where you're not wanted.
