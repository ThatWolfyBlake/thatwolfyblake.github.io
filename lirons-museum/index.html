Lore

Liron's Museum, the place where children could learn about many different things... from history, astrology, computing, biology, cooking, gardening and much more. There was a character that represented the subject for each area of the museum.

But after the budget ran out... the handlers and animators were fired... and the show was shut down...

in a few days the museum was also shut down.

The place was locked, to never open again.

The toons were forgotten, alone in the museum.

Now it's up to you to uncover the secrets of this place.

Now the museum is only a memory to the people who visited it as children...
